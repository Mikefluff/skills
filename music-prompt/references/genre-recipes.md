# Genre recipes

12 paste-ready recipes. Each carries: target model(s), BPM, key signal, NL style box, lyrics box with embedded `[Section | tag | tag | tag]` stacks, and notes. Tags are EN. Lyrics may be EN or RU — language switch at section boundary only.

---

## Hyperpop (2020-present)

**Target models**: Suno v5.5, Udio v4
**BPM**: 150-170
**Key signal**: pitched-up vocals, glitchy maximalism, saw-bass squelch, sugar-overdrive

### Style box (Suno)

```
Hyperpop, pitched-up female vocal, distorted 808s, saw-bass squelch, chiptune flourishes, glitchy maximalism, sugar-overdrive, ~160 BPM.
```

### Lyrics box with embedded tags (Suno)

```
[Intro | hyperpop | pitched-up vocal chops | glitch fx | 160 BPM]

[Verse 1 | hyperpop verse | distorted 808 | saw bass | pitched female vocal]
I melt my screen into a candy-colored scream
Heart-rate hits the ceiling like a bedroom rave dream

[Chorus | hyperpop chorus | wall of sugar synths | pitched harmonies | shouted ad-libs]
Crash me out, crash me out, crash me out tonight
Static in my chest, neon in my eyes
(yeah! crash!)

[Outro | tape stop | pitched-down vocal | bitcrush fade]
```

### Notes / variations
- Swap pitched-up female for pitched-up male if the topline sits below E4
- Common mistake: writing it at 130 BPM — too slow, kills the maximalism
- Add `glitched vocal chops` to the chorus stack if Suno underdelivers on chaos

## Drill / UK drill (2018-present)

**Target models**: Suno v5.5
**BPM**: 138-150
**Key signal**: sliding 808s, dark menacing piano, hi-hat triplets, syncopated kick, half-time feel

### Style box (Suno)

```
UK drill, sliding 808s, dark menacing piano motif, hi-hat triplets, syncopated kick, half-time feel, male rap vocal, ~140 BPM, low-end heavy.
```

### Lyrics box with embedded tags (Suno)

```
[Intro | drill | dark piano motif | sliding 808 | 140 BPM]

[Verse 1 | UK drill | male rap vocal | hi-hat triplets | half-time]
Block-cold winter, breath cuts the air like a blade

[Hook | drill hook | doubled vocal | sliding 808 emphasis | dark]
Same road, same rain, same name on the wall

[Verse 2 | drill verse | aggressive flow | menacing piano | sub bass]
```

## Cross-genre pitfalls

1. **BPM mismatch** — naming a genre then giving a tempo outside its window (hyperpop at 130, hardcore at 140) silently breaks the recipe. Always check the genre's BPM range first.
2. **Hybrid stacking** — "orchestral hyperpop drill" produces mush. Pick one anchor genre and use modifiers, not parallel genres.
3. **Vague style words** — "good vocals", "nice production", "atmospheric" do nothing. Replace with named instrument + named role + named texture.
4. **Mid-line language switch** — "I melt my screen 어둠 속에서 scream" mispronounces in every model. Switch only at section boundaries.
5. **Wrong model for length** — asking Suno for a 9-minute coherent jazz piece; Suno tops out around 8 min and loses thread past 5. Use Udio v4 for long-form.
6. **No section structure** — handing a Lyrics box without `[Intro]`, `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Outro]` brackets lets Suno guess. Always section it.
7. **Negative-by-positive** — saying "no drums" while listing `four-on-floor` in the tag stack. The tag stack wins. Remove the contradiction, don't argue with it.
