# Lynch · dream static

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *Twin Peaks*, *Mulholland Drive*, *Lost Highway*, *Blue Velvet*, *Eraserhead*, and *Inland Empire*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Mid-century-decay interiors lit by single sodium-amber or sickly green practicals, with deep plugged shadows swallowing the frame edges. Red velvet curtains, checkerboard floors, and 1950s domestic surfaces recur as set elements. Camera is locked off for unsettling stretches, occasionally executing a single glacial zoom into a face or object that takes 15-25 seconds to complete. Lighting is hard and from below or from a single ceiling fixture, modeling faces in ways that hover between glamour and grotesque. Smoke fills the air, catching every beam. The grade is warm-amber for interiors and ice-blue for exteriors, with skin pushed slightly waxy. Composition is symmetrical but slightly off — a centered subject with one detail asymmetrical enough to feel wrong. Frames feel like a dream half-remembered.

**Color palette**: sodium amber, deep red velvet, sickly green, ice-blue exterior, plugged black.

**Lens & framing**: 40mm and 50mm spherical primes, medium DoF, 1.85:1 flat, symmetrical composition with one deliberate asymmetry, frequent low-angle wide of a single-source interior.

**Lighting**: single hard practical (ceiling fixture, table lamp, sodium streetlight, refrigerator interior) with no fill, smoke filling the air catching the beam. Up-light from below for grotesque faces, green sickly fluorescent for hallways and bathrooms. Plugged black at frame edges.

**Motion language**: locked tripod by default. Single glacial zoom into a face or object over 15-25 seconds. Slow dolly drift toward a doorway. Camera moves only when the dream demands it.

**Editing rhythm**: slow with sudden ruptures — 3-6 cuts per minute baseline, occasionally cutting hard to an unexplained image (a black mass, a flickering bulb, a stranger's face). Cuts on a sound cue or on the completion of a glacial zoom.

**Shot anchor (per-shot prompt fragment)**:
> 50mm spherical prime, medium depth of field, 1.85:1 flat aspect. Single hard practical light source — ceiling fixture, table lamp, sodium streetlight or refrigerator interior — with no fill of any other source, smoke or haze filling the air to catch the beam visibly. Mid-century-decay interior with red velvet curtains, checkerboard floor or 1950s domestic surfaces. Warm sodium-amber grade with deep plugged-black shadows swallowing the frame edges, skin pushed slightly waxy. Symmetrical centered composition with one deliberate asymmetry (a tilted picture, an open drawer, a single object out of place) creating quiet wrongness. Camera locked on a sturdy tripod or executing a single glacial zoom toward a face or object over the full shot duration. Atmosphere uncanny, dreamlike, slow-burn, suspended outside time.

**Action vocabulary**:
- Locked symmetrical wide of a domestic interior, single subject motionless in mid-distance
- Glacial zoom from wide to extreme close-up of a face over 20 seconds, no other motion
- Subject sits at a kitchen table under a single ceiling pendant, smoke rising from a cigarette
- Slow dolly drift toward an open doorway revealing red velvet curtains and darkness beyond
- Locked low-angle of a ceiling fixture humming, frame held until the bulb flickers
- Insert: a single object in extreme close-up — a key, a lipstick, a phone — bathed in sodium light
- Subject turns to lens with a delayed beat, expression unreadable, eyes catching the light
- Hallway lit by sickly green fluorescent, distant figure visible at the end, frame holds 8 seconds
- Locked wide of a stage with red curtains, single subject standing center in a follow-spot
- Subject speaks in mid-shot, dialogue delivered at unusual cadence, pauses unnaturally long
- Glacial push toward a window with sheer curtains, exterior streetlight glowing through
- Hard cut to an unexplained image (mass of black, flickering filament, stranger's face) held 3 seconds

**Sound design implications**: sound design-led — low rumble, room tone amplified, ventilation hum, distant industrial drone always present underneath. Dialogue at unusual cadence, sometimes slowed, sometimes reversed. Score is sparse — vintage doo-wop ballads, jazz lounge, or a slow organ chord stretched across minutes.

**Best for**: surreal brand mood piece, uncanny narrative micro-film, dreamlike product reveal, art-direction showcase.

**Avoid for**: tutorial content, dialogue-heavy interview, kinetic action, anything light or comedic.

**Suggested duration**: 1-shot × 12s for a single dream-tableau, or 2-shot × 10s for an uncanny diptych.

**Suggested music style**: industrial-drone.
