# Villeneuve · monumental sci-fi

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *Blade Runner 2049*, *Dune*, *Dune Part Two*, *Arrival*, and *Sicario*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Anamorphic widescreen compositions built around scale — single human figures dwarfed against monumental brutalist architecture, vast desert horizons, or fog-haze interiors that erase walls and ceilings. Lighting is monochromatic per-scene, the entire frame committed to a single tonal family: amber-ochre dust, sterile cyan interior, blood-orange catastrophe sky. Atmospheric haze is omnipresent, giving every light source a visible volumetric beam. Camera is locked or executing a single slow push toward a vanishing point. Composition centers a tiny figure against enormous negative space — sky, wall, floor — using architecture as character. Lenses are wide anamorphic with characteristic horizontal flare and 2.39:1 ratio. Production design favors raw concrete, sand, monolithic geometry, and minimalist costuming. Frames feel solemn, geographic, ceremonial.

**Color palette**: ochre-amber dust, sterile cyan, blood-orange, slate concrete, deep black.

**Lens & framing**: anamorphic 2.39:1 widescreen on 35mm-equivalent primes, deep focus, low-and-wide hero angles, figure placed small inside enormous negative space, frequent symmetrical compositions of architecture.

**Lighting**: monochromatic per-scene — entire frame committed to one tonal family (ochre dust, sterile cyan, blood-orange). Atmospheric haze omnipresent, giving every source a visible volumetric beam. Single hard sun or single hard practical, no fill, deep shadows accepted.

**Motion language**: locked tripod or single slow push toward a vanishing point over 15-25 seconds. Aerial wide for geography establishment. Vehicle-mounted slow track parallel to architecture. No handheld, no whip pans.

**Editing rhythm**: very slow, 3-6 cuts per minute. Long held wides for architectural reveal. Cuts on a sub-bass drone hit or a quiet inflection of light. Match-cuts on color (one scene's amber bleeding into the next).

**Action vocabulary**:
- Locked wide of monumental architecture, single tiny figure walking across the frame at distance
- Slow push toward a vanishing-point doorway, single figure framed dead-center inside it
- Aerial descent from sky to ground-level revealing a vast desert expanse with figure visible
- Subject walks toward camera through fog-haze interior, becoming visible only at medium distance
- Locked low-angle of brutalist column, single figure standing at its base for scale
- Sterile cyan interior, two figures at opposite ends of a long corridor, mirror-symmetrical
- Sand cascades across the foreground, distant figure visible through the falling grains
- Slow vehicle-mounted track parallel to a monolithic wall, scale unfolding over 12 seconds
- Subject silhouetted against a blood-orange catastrophe sky, no other detail in frame
- Monumental practical effect (collapse, explosion, ship descent) at distance, figure watches in foreground
- Tight close-up on subject's eyes inside a helmet visor, reflection of architecture in the glass
- Match-cut on color: amber dust fills frame, then ocean-cyan fills frame in the next shot

**Sound design implications**: sub-bass drone wall-to-wall, sparse percussion of metal and stone, low brass swells. Dialogue is whispered, sparse, often subtitled or in invented language. Foley emphasizes scale — boots on stone, fabric flapping in wind, distant rumble.

**Best for**: monumental brand reveal, sci-fi narrative trailer, architectural showcase, austere luxury launch.

**Avoid for**: comedy, intimate dialogue, kinetic action, anything requiring warmth or human-scale closeness.

**Suggested duration**: 3-shot × 8s for an architectural reveal, or 1-shot × 12s for a single monumental tableau.

**Suggested music style**: sub-bass-drone.
