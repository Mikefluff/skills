# Soderbergh · natural light handheld

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *Traffic*, *Out of Sight*, *Contagion*, *Magic Mike*, *Side Effects*, *Erin Brockovich*, *High Flying Bird*, and *The Limey*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Available-light photography of real locations, treating fiction with documentary discipline. The camera is operated by a single person doubling as cinematographer, executing loose handheld coverage that drifts and reframes restlessly, finding the moment rather than blocking for it. Lenses are spherical primes, often wide, with shallow DoF that picks subjects out of busy real-world backgrounds. The grade is naturalistic with mild color separation by location — a corporate scene leans cyan-grey, a domestic scene leans amber-warm, a clinical scene leans sterile green — but skin tones remain real. No movie-light, no make-up, no production-design intervention beyond what the real location offers. Wardrobe is lived-in, hair is unstyled. Composition is loose and reactive, often with a foreground obstruction (a passing extra, a doorway) breaking the frame.

**Color palette**: location-real — cyan-grey corporate, amber-warm domestic, sterile fluorescent green, naturalistic skin.

**Lens & framing**: spherical primes 24mm/32mm/40mm, shallow DoF picking subject out of busy background, 1.85:1 flat, loose reactive framing with occasional foreground obstruction, eye-level for everyone.

**Lighting**: available light only — window light, real overhead office fluorescent, real domestic lamp. No movie lights, no bounce, no diffusion. Color cast left untouched (fluorescents read green, tungsten reads amber, daylight reads cool).

**Motion language**: loose handheld throughout, single operator. Restless reframes drifting from one subject to another mid-shot. Slow walk-and-talk follows alongside subject. Occasional locked-off security-camera-style wide for variety.

**Editing rhythm**: medium tempo, 12-18 cuts per minute. Cuts often mid-action or mid-sentence — overlapping dialogue runs across the cut. Match-cuts on similar action or color. Time-jumps inside the same scene allowed.

**Shot anchor (per-shot prompt fragment)**:

Frequent foreground obstruction (passing figure, doorway, partial wall) breaking the composition. Atmosphere observational, documentary, fly-on-wall.

**Action vocabulary**:
- Walk-and-talk handheld alongside subject moving through a real corridor or street
- Loose two-shot dialogue, camera drifts from one face to the other mid-line
- Subject works at a desk under real overhead fluorescent, camera holds at slight distance
- Shot through a doorway with passing extra crossing the foreground unscripted-looking
- Locked-off wide of a real public space, subject enters from screen-right and is found by camera
- Tight handheld over-the-shoulder of subject scrolling a phone or device, screen visible
- Camera follows subject into a room, pauses at the threshold while subject continues
- Mid-action cut: subject is mid-sentence, the next shot picks up in a different room mid-sentence
- Insert: hands counting cash, hands sorting pills, hands typing — close handheld, real lighting
- Subject in motion (running, climbing stairs, exiting a car), camera tracks at near-real distance
- Two subjects in a vehicle, dashboard-mounted lock-off, real ambient road light
- Reaction shot: subject listening, half-attention elsewhere, frame holds 4 seconds before cut

**Sound design implications**: dialogue is naturalistic, overlapping, sometimes hard to hear — real production sound. Ambient location wash foregrounded (HVAC, traffic, crowd murmur). Score is electronic and minimal — synth pulse, occasional jazz piano. Music often plays under and across cuts as connective tissue.

**Best for**: documentary-style brand reel, founder interview, real-location product testimonial, gritty narrative.

**Avoid for**: high-gloss luxury, choreographed musical, surveillance thriller, controlled-studio product shot.

**Suggested duration**: 3-shot × 6s for a walk-and-talk reel, or 2-shot × 8s for a documentary moment.

**Suggested music style**: electronic-minimal.
