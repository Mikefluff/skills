# Refn · neon static

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *Drive*, *The Neon Demon*, *Only God Forgives*, *Too Old to Die Young*, and *Valhalla Rising*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Glacially-paced fashion-grade tableaux drenched in saturated single-color light — pure magenta, pure cyan, pure cobalt — with subjects posed motionless as mannequins inside locked symmetrical frames. Lighting comes from large LED panels gelled to one wavelength, the entire frame bathed in that hue with no fill of any other color. Faces are lit flat and frontal, every blemish rendered as fashion-editorial perfection. Camera is locked off or executing a single glacial dolly-in over 20-40 seconds, with imperceptible reframes. Composition is rigorously symmetrical, often shot from a low-and-centered hero angle that turns subjects into icons. Action is sparse and ritualized — a head turn, a hand raised, a step forward — staged like a runway pose rather than naturalistic behavior. Atmosphere is hypnotic, ceremonial, lethal.

**Color palette**: magenta, cyan, cobalt blue, blood red, polished black.

**Lens & framing**: 50mm and 75mm spherical primes, medium-to-shallow DoF, 2.00:1 or 2.39:1 widescreen, rigorously symmetrical centered composition, low-and-centered hero angles, 90-degree overhead inserts for ritual objects.

**Lighting**: large LED panels gelled to a single saturated wavelength (pure magenta, pure cyan, pure cobalt), bathing the entire frame in that hue with no contrasting fill. Flat frontal key on the face. Hard rim light from behind in the same hue or its complement.

**Motion language**: locked tripod by default. Single glacial dolly-in over 20-40 seconds. Slow 180-degree pan revealing a second motionless tableau. Overhead 90-degree-down for ritual inserts. No handheld, no whip pans.

**Editing rhythm**: very slow, 2-5 cuts per minute, individual shots held 8-20 seconds. Cuts are clean, often on a hard ambient sound cue. No coverage cutting within a scene — each tableau is one shot.

**Shot anchor (per-shot prompt fragment)**:
> 50mm spherical prime, medium depth of field, 2.39:1 widescreen aspect, rigorously symmetrical centered composition with the subject placed dead on the optical axis. Single-wavelength saturated lighting — pure magenta, pure cyan, or pure cobalt blue from large LED panels — bathing the entire frame in that hue with no contrasting fill. Flat frontal key on the face rendering skin as fashion-editorial perfection, hard rim light from behind in the same hue or its complement. Locked tripod or single glacial dolly-in executed over the full duration of the shot. Subject posed motionless as a mannequin, expression vacant, gesture ritualized like a runway pose rather than naturalistic. Atmosphere hypnotic, ceremonial, lethally still.

**Action vocabulary**:
- Subject stands motionless, dead-centered, bathed in pure magenta light, frame holds 10 seconds
- Glacial dolly-in over 20 seconds toward a stationary subject's face, no other motion in frame
- Slow 180-degree pan from one motionless tableau to a second motionless tableau in adjacent space
- Subject turns head slowly to lens, eye contact held without expression
- Overhead 90-degree-down insert of hands placing a ritual object on a polished surface
- Two subjects face each other across the frame, mirror-symmetrical, neither moves
- Subject walks slowly toward camera through a corridor lit by a single cobalt wall-wash
- Locked wide of an empty room saturated in pure cyan, subject enters from screen-right, stops dead-center
- Ritual reveal: cloth slowly pulled away from an object on a pedestal, frame symmetrical
- Subject raises a single hand to the camera, gesture frozen, light shifts from magenta to red
- Reflection shot: subject in front of a mirror, both subject and reflection lit identically
- Slow descent from overhead reveal: camera drops from ceiling to floor level over 15 seconds

**Sound design implications**: minimal foley, dialogue almost absent. Score is dominated by analog synth drones, vintage drum machines, and a single repeating arpeggio that pulses for the entire duration. Sudden ambient cues (a door slam, a struck bell) used as percussive punctuation.

**Best for**: high-fashion brand reveal, ritualistic product launch, hypnotic mood piece, luxury automotive.

**Avoid for**: explainer content, dialogue-driven narrative, anything kinetic or playful, daylight outdoor.

**Suggested duration**: 1-shot × 12s for a single tableau, or 2-shot × 10s for a paired ritual.

**Suggested music style**: synth-arpeggio.
