# Fincher · cold low-key

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *Se7en*, *Zodiac*, *The Social Network*, *Gone Girl*, *Mindhunter*, and *Fight Club*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Low-key digital photography with deep, plugged blacks and a desaturated, cool, slightly greenish-teal cast offset by amber tungsten practicals. Lighting is hard and motivated, almost always from a single source — a desk lamp, an overhead office fluorescent, a window — with no fill on the shadow side, so half the face falls into near-black. Camera is locked off on a fluid head, executing microscopic moves: 6-12 inch dolly-ins held over twenty seconds, near-imperceptible reframes, no shake. Framing is symmetrical and architectural, often shooting through doorways or down hallways to compress space. Lenses are anamorphic or wide spherical primes; depth of field is shallow at the focal plane but the background still reads as a deliberate object. Composition is meticulous, the frame feels engineered rather than discovered.

**Color palette**: deep teal, amber tungsten, desaturated flesh, black, gunmetal grey.

**Lens & framing**: anamorphic 2.39:1 or spherical 2.0:1 primes (32mm, 40mm, 50mm), shallow DoF on the focal plane, symmetrical composition shot down hallways or through door frames, eye-level for interrogations, slightly low for power-figures.

**Lighting**: single hard motivated source (overhead fluorescent, desk lamp, monitor glow), key/fill ratio 8:1 or higher, deep plugged blacks below eye line, amber tungsten practicals balanced against teal ambience, no bounce.

**Motion language**: locked tripod. Imperceptible 6-12 inch dolly-ins or push-outs over 15-25 seconds. Slow track parallel to a walking subject down a hallway. No handheld, no whip pans, no zooms.

**Editing rhythm**: slow, methodical, 4-8 cuts per minute. Long takes that hold past comfort. Cuts on a precise eye-line shift or a beat of silence rather than action.

**Shot anchor (per-shot prompt fragment)**:
> Anamorphic 2.39:1 widescreen, 40mm prime look, shallow depth of field with the focal plane locked on the subject's near eye. Low-key lighting from a single hard motivated source — overhead fluorescent, desk lamp or monitor glow — with no fill, half the face falling into plugged near-black shadow. Cool desaturated palette of deep teal, amber tungsten and grey, digital sensor cleanliness, no film grain. Architectural symmetrical composition, framed down a hallway or through a doorway, perspective lines converging dead-center. Camera locked on a fluid head executing only a microscopic 6-to-12-inch dolly-in over the duration of the shot, no shake, no handheld. Subject still, eyes precise, breath visible. Atmosphere clinical, methodical, surveillance-grade.

**Action vocabulary**:
- Locked symmetrical wide of a hallway, subject walks slowly toward camera, frame holds
- Six-inch micro dolly-in on a seated subject's face over 20 seconds, no other motion
- Over-the-shoulder interrogation two-shot, focal plane on the speaker, background subject soft
- Single hard overhead practical (fluorescent or pendant), subject seated directly beneath it
- Insert: hands typing on a keyboard, monitor glow uplighting fingers, no other light
- Locked wide of a desk in a darkened room, single amber lamp on, chair empty then occupied
- Slow track parallel to subject walking down a corridor, parallax revealing depth
- Match-cut on a precise object (file, photograph, screen) handed between two shots
- Reveal pull-out: tight on a screen, slow dolly back through a doorway to wider context
- Subject seated, half-face lit, half-face plugged black, slow blink, no other movement
- Static two-shot, two subjects at opposite ends of a long table, perspective compressed
- Push-in to a monitor, then through-the-monitor POV match cut to what's on screen

**Sound design implications**: ambient room tone foregrounded — fluorescent hum, HVAC, distant traffic. Dialogue is dry, close-miked, minimal reverb. Score is restrained — pulsing low synth drones, minor-key piano, almost subliminal. Silence is weaponized.

**Best for**: tense interrogation reel, methodical product-process explainer, surveillance-aesthetic crime narrative, corporate thriller monologue.

**Avoid for**: comedy, dance/music videos, kinetic sports, anything requiring warmth or improvisation.

**Suggested duration**: 2-shot × 8s for an interrogation beat, or 1-shot × 12s for a slow-burn monologue.

**Suggested music style**: ambient-drone.
