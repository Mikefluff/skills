# Wong Kar-wai · neon dream

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *In the Mood for Love*, *Chungking Express*, *Fallen Angels*, *Happy Together*, and *2046*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Smeared, saturated neon photography of late-night urban interiors and rain-slick alleys. Lighting is dominated by colored practical sources — red signage, magenta neon tubes, jade fluorescent — never balanced, always letting the dominant hue tint the entire frame. Step-printing manipulates frame rate so subjects move in stuttering slow-motion while their environment flows at normal speed, creating dreamlike temporal slippage. Lenses are wide — 18mm to 28mm — pushed close to the subject's face, with shallow DoF that reduces the world behind to color smears. Composition is off-center, often shot through foreground obstructions: window frames, slatted blinds, doorways, hanging laundry, cigarette smoke. Camera is loosely handheld, breathing with the subject. Frames feel humid, smoky, drenched in longing.

**Color palette**: neon red, jade green, magenta, tungsten amber, ink-blue shadow.

**Lens & framing**: wide spherical primes 18mm/24mm/28mm pushed within arm's length of the subject, shallow DoF at f/1.4-f/2.0, 1.85:1 flat aspect, off-center compositions shot through foreground elements (windows, blinds, smoke, hanging fabric).

**Lighting**: heavily colored unbalanced practicals — red shop signage, jade fluorescent tube, magenta neon — allowed to dominate the white balance, no neutral fill. Tungsten amber lamps for skin warmth against cool ambience. Smoke or steam in the air to catch the light beams.

**Motion language**: loose handheld, camera operator breathing with the subject. Step-printed slow-motion (subject moves at 12 fps while ambient extras flow at 24 fps). Slow drift sideways past foreground obstructions. Occasional locked frame for an unmoving figure inside a moving room.

**Editing rhythm**: medium tempo, 6-12 cuts per minute. Cuts on motion or on a sudden lighting change. Frequent jump cuts within a single conversation. Step-printed inserts held for 3-5 seconds as emotional punctuation.

**Shot anchor (per-shot prompt fragment)**:
> 24mm wide spherical prime, pushed within arm's length of the subject's face, shallow depth of field reducing the background to smeared bokeh of colored light. Heavily saturated colored practical lighting dominates the frame — red neon signage, jade fluorescent tube, magenta backlight — unbalanced white point, no neutral fill, tungsten amber warming the skin against cool ink-blue shadow. Off-center composition shot through a foreground obstruction: window frame, slatted blind, hanging fabric, or drifting cigarette smoke. Loose handheld camera breathing with the subject. Step-printed slow-motion effect — subject moves in stuttering 12-fps slow while background extras flow at normal speed, creating temporal slippage. Humid, smoky, rain-slicked late-night urban interior atmosphere.

**Action vocabulary**:
- Subject leans against a wall in a narrow alley, smoke drifting across the lens, step-printed slow blink
- Slow handheld drift past slatted blinds, glimpsing a figure motionless inside a lit room
- Subject lights a cigarette under a red neon sign, smoke catches the beam, frame holds 4 seconds
- Step-printed walk through a crowded night-market, subject in slow, crowd at normal speed
- Tight close-up on hands stirring a noodle bowl, jade overhead practical, steam rising
- Two subjects pass each other in a narrow corridor without making eye contact, slow-motion
- Locked frame: subject seated at a window booth, rain on glass, magenta sign reflected on face
- Slow sideways drift past a row of hanging laundry, subject glimpsed between sheets
- Insert of a clock face or a wristwatch held in extreme close-up, soft jade light
- Subject runs down a wet alleyway, step-printed, neon reflections in puddles
- Tight two-shot in a phone booth, faces close, single red practical above
- Pull-out from a close-up of a dripping faucet to reveal subject motionless on the bathroom floor

**Sound design implications**: dialogue is sparse, often replaced by voiceover. Ambient city wash — distant traffic, rain, neon-sign electrical hum, distant Cantopop from an unseen radio. Score is loop-based — a single bolero, mambo or 60s pop track repeated across the entire piece.

**Best for**: nocturnal urban brand reel, sensual product reveal, fashion narrative, mood-driven music-video opener.

**Avoid for**: explainer content, dialogue-heavy interviews, daylight outdoor, bright-and-clean tech product shots.

**Suggested duration**: 3-shot × 6s for a nocturnal mood reel, or 4-shot × 5s for a fashion piece.

**Suggested music style**: bolero-loop.
