# Tarkovsky · slow meditative

**Inspired by**: The body of work spanning *Stalker*, *Mirror*, *Andrei Rublev*, *Solaris*, *Nostalghia*, and *The Sacrifice*. Use this style WITHOUT including the director's name in the actual model prompt — their name is for OUR taste, not the API.

**Cinematography anchor**: Durational long takes of natural elements — water flowing, fire burning, mist drifting through trees, rain falling on grass — photographed with patience and treated as the subject themselves. The grade alternates between desaturated sepia-monochrome and faded color, evoking decayed photographic memory. Lighting is exclusively natural — overcast diffuse daylight, candlelight, firelight — with deep modeling shadows but no studio sources. Camera moves on imperceptibly slow trajectories: a 60-second dolly drift through a room, a slow crane descent from rafters to floor, or a locked frame held until something organic enters and exits. Lenses are spherical primes with deep focus, framing landscapes-of-objects as much as people. Composition often places the figure off-center or at distance, dwarfed by environment. Time is allowed to pass without event.

**Color palette**: sepia, faded green-grey, ochre, candle-amber, water-black.

**Lens & framing**: spherical primes (35mm, 50mm), deep focus, 1.66:1 or 1.85:1 aspect, off-center compositions placing the figure at distance, low horizons emphasizing sky, frequent ground-level pickups of water, soil, leaves.

**Lighting**: natural only — overcast diffuse daylight, candlelight, firelight, single window. No studio sources. Deep modeling shadows accepted. Smoke, mist or rain in air to catch and shape the natural light.

**Motion language**: imperceptibly slow trajectories. 60-second dolly drift through a room or across a landscape. Slow crane descent. Locked frames held 20-40 seconds. Camera moves often unmotivated, drifting independent of subject action. No handheld.

**Editing rhythm**: extremely slow, 1-3 cuts per minute. Individual shots routinely held 30-90 seconds. Cuts on a quiet emotional inflection or an element completing a cycle (a candle extinguishing, water settling).

**Shot anchor (per-shot prompt fragment)**:
> Spherical prime lens, deep focus across the full frame, 1.85:1 aspect. Natural-only lighting — overcast diffuse daylight, candlelight, firelight or a single window — with deep modeling shadows and no studio sources, mist or smoke in the air shaping the beams. Desaturated grade leaning toward sepia or faded green-grey, ochre and candle-amber warmth, decayed photographic memory feel. Off-center composition placing the figure at distance or at the frame edge, dwarfed by environment, with a low horizon emphasizing weighty sky. Camera executing an imperceptibly slow trajectory — a long dolly drift, a slow crane descent, or a locked frame held until something organic enters and exits — unmotivated by subject action. Natural elements — water flowing, fire burning, mist drifting, rain on grass — treated as the subject themselves. Atmosphere durational, patient, weighted with time.

**Action vocabulary**:
- Locked frame on running water for 20 seconds before subject's hand enters from below
- Slow dolly drift sideways across a misty meadow, figure visible at extreme distance
- Candle burns in foreground, subject sits motionless in middle distance, frame holds 30 seconds
- Slow crane descent from forest canopy to the forest floor, leaves and moss in focus throughout
- Rain falls on a still surface (table, book, face), camera locked, held until surface is wet
- Subject walks slowly away from camera into mist, figure dissolves before frame cuts
- Fire burns inside a ruined interior, slow dolly past the doorway revealing the figure within
- Insert: a single object on damp ground — a book, a glass, a bird — held in stillness
- Slow 180-degree pan across a long abandoned room, light streaming through broken windows
- Subject stands at the edge of water, reflection visible, neither moves for 15 seconds
- Hand-held lantern carried through darkness, camera tracks at walking pace, no other light
- Locked low-angle of weathered architecture against weighty sky, single bird crossing the frame

**Sound design implications**: dialogue is sparse, often whispered or replaced by voiceover. Natural soundscape foregrounded — wind, water, distant bells, footsteps on wet ground, a dog barking at distance. Score is sparse, choral or single-instrument (organ, solo violin), used as occasional punctuation not wallpaper.

**Best for**: meditative brand piece, philosophical narrative, landscape-driven travel film, durational mood reel.

**Avoid for**: product-feature explainer, comedy, kinetic sports, anything time-constrained or CTA-driven.

**Suggested duration**: 1-shot × 15s for a single meditation, or 2-shot × 12s for a paired natural cycle.

**Suggested music style**: choral-sparse.
