# Modern gospel

**Vibe**: Hammond B3 swell, full mixed choir, belted female lead carrying a room into testify. Quiet verse builds slowly into a key-change bridge that lifts everything one step higher.

**Era & lineage**: Contemporary gospel (1990s-present) — urban-contemporary gospel era, full-choir + belted-lead + B3 organ template. Lineage: traditional gospel, soul, R&B, CCM.

**Tempo**: 65-85 BPM. **Key tendency**: major, often C, F, Bb, Eb — modulates UP a half-step or whole-step at the bridge (canonical move).

**Core sonic signature**:
- Hammond B3 organ with Leslie cabinet swell
- Full mixed choir (gospel choir, call-and-response)
- Soulful belted female lead vocal (melismatic R&B runs)
- Gospel piano with chromatic runs and walk-ups
- Warm electric bass
- Brushed drums in verse, building to full kit by chorus
- Modulation up a step at bridge — name it explicitly
- Tambourine, hand claps on uptempo sections
- Wide stereo, hall reverb on choir

**Lyrics conventions for this genre**:
- Verse-prechorus-chorus-bridge-chorus, with modulation at bridge
- 4-line verse, 4-line chorus repeated 2-3x with melismatic ad-libs
- Hook word repeated 3x — "lift it up, lift it up, lift it up"
- Ad-libs in parens on own line — `(hallelujah!)` `(testify)` `(oh yes)`
- Imagery: morning, light, mountain, river, name, room, hand, lift, carry
- EN dominant, Black-church vernacular lands ("won't He do it", "thank you Jesus" — only if authentic to the message)
