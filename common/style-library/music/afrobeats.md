# Afrobeats

**Vibe**: Lagos heat at golden hour, mid-tempo groove, sun on her shoulders. Log drum bassline carrying the song, melodic male phrasing on top.

**Era & lineage**: West African pop crossover (Nigerian Afrobeats / Ghanaian Afropop) — 2010s emergence into global streaming dominance 2020-present. Lineage: highlife, juju, Afrobeat (Fela), dancehall.

**Tempo**: 98-108 BPM. **Key tendency**: major, often C, F, G — bright and warm.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Log drum bassline (THE genre stamp — name it explicitly)
- Syncopated shaker + conga percussion bed
- Warm male lead vocal with melodic phrasing (or breathy alto female alternative)
- Mid-tempo groove — never rushed
- Sun-soaked production, slight tape warmth
- Bright synth lead in chorus, often pluck or marimba-like
- Call-and-response group vocal ad-libs
- Pidgin English phrasing common

**Lyrics conventions for this genre**:
- 8-bar verses, 4-line chorus repeated
- Melodic phrasing — sing-song lines, not rapped
- Imagery: sun, ocean, dancing, slow-down, baby, golden, shoulders, laughter
- Pidgin English / Yoruba words land if at section boundary
- Ad-libs in parens — `(ah ah)` `(yeah)`
- Hook repeats 3-4x in chorus
