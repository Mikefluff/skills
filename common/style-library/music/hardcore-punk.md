# Hardcore punk

**Vibe**: Walls of distortion, blast-beat drums, throat-shouted vocal. Under two minutes, no apology, no fade-out.

**Era & lineage**: First wave 1980-1986 (Black Flag, Minor Threat, Bad Brains lineage — referenced by era, not name), 90s NYHC, 2000s melodic / chaotic hardcore, 2020s revival. Adjacent to thrash, crust, powerviolence.

**Tempo**: 170-200 BPM. **Key tendency**: minor or modal (Em, Dm, drop-D power chords). Often atonal in breakdowns.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Fast distorted guitars (power chords, palm-muted chugs)
- Blast-beat drums, double-kick patterns
- Shouted male vocal (or screamed — never sung clean except melodic hardcore variant)
- Raw garage recording aesthetic — no polish
- Gang vocals on chorus shouts
- Half-time breakdowns with palm-mute chug
- Sub-2-minute total runtime — anything longer becomes metalcore
- Feedback squeal intro, abrupt stop outro

**Lyrics conventions for this genre**:
- 8-16 bar verses, 4-line shouted chorus, breakdown call-out line
- Short declarative lines, no metaphor stacking
- All-caps lines for shouts, lowercase for verse mutter
- Gang vocals on chorus — write as `(WE SHOUT IT BACK)` on its own line
- Imagery: walls, doors, teeth, floor, fire, sirens, refusal
- EN dominant — single-syllable verbs work best
