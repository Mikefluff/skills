# Jazz fusion

**Vibe**: Rhodes comping in 7/8 over a fretless bass that walks like it's thinking out loud. Brushed drums keep the groove honest while solos take long, patient turns.

**Era & lineage**: 1969-1980 first wave (modal-electric jazz, Rhodes + odd-meter + extended chords era), 1980s smooth-fusion offshoot, 2010s neo-fusion revival (large-ensemble groove fusion).

**Tempo**: 95-130 BPM. **Key tendency**: modal (Dorian, Lydian, Mixolydian) — minor 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 extended chords. Often Dm9, Cmaj9, Fmaj7#11.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Rhodes electric piano comping
- Fretless bass with melodic runs (or upright bass on traditional sections)
- Brushed drums, often in odd meter (7/8, 5/4, 9/8)
- Extended chords — never plain triads
- Instrumental — vocals rare and short if used
- Tempo shifts between the head (theme) and solos
- Long form — solos need room to develop
- Saxophone or trumpet solo as featured voice
- Wide stereo, room reverb, analog warmth
