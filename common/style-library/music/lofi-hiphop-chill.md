# Lo-fi hip-hop chill

**Vibe**: Late-night study desk, rain on the window, instant coffee gone cold. Dusty piano loop drifting on top of muted boom-bap, vinyl crackle holding the whole thing together.

**Era & lineage**: J Dilla / Nujabes lineage through 2010s Soulection / ChilledCow / 24-hour-livestream culture. Stabilized as instrumental loop genre 2017-present.

**Tempo**: 75-90 BPM. **Key tendency**: minor seventh / major seventh extended jazz chords — Dm7, Cmaj7, Am9 territory. Mode-mixed, never aggressive.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Dusty piano loop or chopped sample as the central idea
- Vinyl crackle layer (constant, low)
- Muted boom-bap drums (kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, gently swung)
- Jazzy Rhodes electric piano chords
- Soft tape hiss / tape saturation
- Instrumental — vocals collapse the genre into bedroom pop
- Occasional sampled trumpet, sax, or field-recording layer (rain, cafe, cassette click)
