# UK drill

**Vibe**: Concrete winter, breath cutting the air, phone-on-silent stairwell. Cold piano motif over sliding 808s and a half-time strut.

**Era & lineage**: Born in South London 2012-2014 as a UK mutation of Chicago drill. Hard-anchored from 2018 onward, with Brooklyn drill branching off ~2019 (faster, snappier).

**Tempo**: 138-150 BPM (felt half-time). **Key tendency**: minor, often Fm, Gm, or Am — sometimes Phrygian for extra menace.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Sliding 808s — pitch-bent sub kicks doing melodic work
- Dark menacing piano motif as the central hook
- Hi-hat triplets, often off-grid syncopated
- Syncopated kick pattern, half-time snare on the 3
- Male rap vocal, doubled on hook
- Sub bass heavy — low-end is the song's center of gravity
- No reverb on the lead vocal — dry, in-your-face
