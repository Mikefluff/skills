# Modern country

**Vibe**: Two-lane highway, dust on the dash, jacket in the truck. Pedal steel sliding behind a storytelling vocal — restraint over production muscle.

**Era & lineage**: 2010s Nashville post-bro evolution into 2020s neotraditional + country-pop crossover. Lineage: 70s outlaw country, 90s Garth-era radio, 2010s arena country.

**Tempo**: 90-115 BPM. **Key tendency**: major — often G, D, A, C. Minor occasional for heartbreak ballads.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Warm acoustic guitar foundation (strummed open chords)
- Pedal steel slides — the genre-stamp
- Brushed snare on verses, sticks-on-rim on softer sections
- Dobro fills, soft slide guitar
- Storytelling male vocal with light twang (or breathy alto female alternative)
- Walking acoustic bass
- Restraint over density — country is what's NOT in the mix
