# Synthwave

**Vibe**: Neon-lit night drive, palm-tree silhouettes against a magenta-blue gradient. Analog Juno bass, arpeggiated synths, gated reverb snare — instrumental, no vocals.

**Era & lineage**: 80s aesthetic source (1982-1989: analog-synth film score and electronic-pop era). Revival genre 2010-present (outrun / darksynth / dreamwave wave). Subgenres: outrun, darksynth, dreamwave.

**Tempo**: 95-120 BPM. **Key tendency**: minor (Am, Em, Cm) — nocturnal, slightly melancholy.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Analog Juno bass (warm subtractive synth bassline)
- Arpeggiated DX7 / FM synths
- Gated reverb snare (THE 80s signature — name it)
- Electronic toms (Linn-Drum-style)
- DX7 lead synth for melody
- Neon-city night-drive imagery in production color
- Instrumental — vocals shift it into dreamwave / synthpop subgenre
- VHS warmth, slight tape saturation, wide stereo
