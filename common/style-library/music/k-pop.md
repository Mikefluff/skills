# K-Pop

**Vibe**: Mirror in the elevator, lipstick as a warning, the city holding its breath. Polished synth-pop verse rolling into an EDM-drop chorus with EN/KO code-switch.

**Era & lineage**: 4th-generation K-Pop (2020-present) — SM, HYBE, JYP, YG studio system, multi-section song architecture, beat-switches, glossy hybrid production. Lineage: 90s J-Pop, 2000s Korean R&B, 2010s western EDM.

**Tempo**: 120-135 BPM. **Key tendency**: minor verse lifting to relative major drop, often F#m → A or Bm → D.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Synth pluck verses with tight drum loops
- EDM drop chorus (four-on-floor, sidechained synths, big lead)
- Female group vocal with stacked harmonies (or boy-group equivalent)
- English / Korean code-switch at section boundaries only
- Beat-switch — bridges often shift to half-time or trap
- Snap FX, finger-click percussion
- Multi-section architecture — pre-chorus matters as much as chorus
- Polished, streaming-optimized mix

**Lyrics conventions for this genre**:
- Verse-prechorus-chorus structure, repeated, plus a bridge with beat-switch
- Code-switch ONLY at section boundary — never mid-line
- Write Korean in Hangul (한글) — TTS mispronounces romanized
- Ad-libs in parens — `(hey! hey!)` `(let's go)`
- Hook line repeats 3x for memorability
- Imagery: mirror, neon, city lights, lipstick, midnight, fire, ice
