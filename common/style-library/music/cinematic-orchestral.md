# Cinematic orchestral

**Vibe**: Soaring violins climbing toward a brass-and-taiko peak — the score behind the moment a character decides to act.

**Era & lineage**: Classical-Hollywood (Korngold, Williams) through modern trailer-music studios (Two Steps From Hell era, 2008-present) and contemporary film-score language (low-brass risers, hybrid orchestral-electronic).

**Tempo**: 80-110 BPM. **Key tendency**: minor for tension, lifts to relative major at climax. Often Dm or Cm with C/Eb-major release.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Soaring violin section as the melodic lead
- Brass swell (horns, low brass) for impact
- Low taiko drums for ground-shaking pulse
- Wordless choir OHHs / AAHs at climax
- Tension-release dynamics (long quiet build into triumphant peak)
- Cinematic strings rise as transition device
- Sparse piano theme in intro / outro
- Wide stereo, hall reverb
