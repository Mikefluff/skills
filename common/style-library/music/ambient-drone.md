# Ambient drone

**Vibe**: A room slowly filling with light. No rhythmic drive, no narrative arc — pure texture and breath, slowly evolving across minutes.

**Era & lineage**: Brian Eno / Harold Budd lineage (late 70s onward) through Stars of the Lid, William Basinski, Tim Hecker. Stabilized as a genre 2000-present.

**Tempo**: 50-70 BPM (functionally beatless — BPM mainly governs swell timing). **Key tendency**: modal, often Dorian / Mixolydian. No clear tonic — drones live between.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Ethereal evolving synth pad as the bed
- Distant bowed strings (cello or violin, sometimes both)
- Sub-bass drone underneath
- Analog tape warmth, granular grain noise
- No percussion — name this explicitly or the model invents drums
- Slow swell and decay across 20-60 second arcs
- Occasional sparse piano note or field recording (wind, ocean, room tone)
- Reverb-soaked, very long tails
