# Hyperpop

**Vibe**: A Y2K bedroom rave melted into a candy machine — pitched-up vocals, saw-bass squelch, sugar overdrive. Every element fights every other element for the front of the mix on purpose.

**Era & lineage**: PC Music label (2014-2018) into the SoundCloud / TikTok wave (2019-present). Direct lineage: glitch pop, J-pop, eurodance, chiptune, trap.

**Tempo**: 150-170 BPM. **Key tendency**: minor, often Em or F#m, with major-lift choruses.

**Core sonic signature**:
- Pitched-up female (or male) vocal, often +3 to +6 semitones
- Saw-bass squelch — detuned supersaw used as bass
- Distorted 808 kicks layered with sub
- Glitch FX, bitcrushed vocal chops, stutter edits
- Chiptune flourishes (8-bit lead, NES blips)
- Wall-of-sugar synth stacks in the chorus
- Tape stop / pitched-down outros

**Lyrics conventions for this genre**:
- Verse-chorus-verse-bridge-chorus, short — total 1:30-2:10
- Verses ~6-8 lines, choruses ~4 lines repeated with ad-lib stacks
- Ad-libs in parens on their own line — `(crash! yeah!)`
- EN-dominant; JP / KR insertions land if at section boundary
- Imagery: candy, neon, melting screens, bedroom raves, crying-laughing emoji feel
