# Flat vector illustration

**Vibe**: The visual grammar of a Series-B SaaS landing page in 2022 — competent, optimistic, slightly anonymous, built to explain a workflow without scaring anyone.

**Palette**: One brand primary (electric blue or coral), one supporting secondary (cool slate or warm mustard), neutral off-white background (F8F6F2), occasional soft pink or mint accent, deep navy for outlines.

**Typography**: Geometric sans with a humanist edge. Reference faces — Inter, Söhne, GT America, Aktiv Grotesk. Headline at semibold, caption at regular, numbers tabular. Always sentence case, never all-caps.

**Medium**: Flat 2D vector — no gradients beyond two stops, no drop shadow, occasional 1px outline on key shapes. Characters drawn as simple geometric figures with rounded joints, oversized hands, no facial features beyond two dots and a curve.

**Composition**: Single-focus illustration on a flat field, headline stacked above or to the left, supporting iconography arranged on an invisible grid, generous breathing room around the main subject. Slide-to-slide consistency through repeated character or object.

**Best for**: SaaS explainers, B2B onboarding decks, product education, feature announcements.

**Avoid for**: luxury brands, fashion, anything that wants to feel handmade or rare.

**Caption tone**: Clear, benefit-led, second-person. Short sentences. One concrete number per slide when possible.
