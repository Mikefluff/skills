# Hand-drawn pastel

**Vibe**: A coach's whiteboard photographed on an iPad — Procreate brush strokes, gentle wobble in every line, the sense that a real person drew this in twenty minutes during a podcast.

**Palette**: Soft peach, butter yellow, sage, periwinkle, dusty coral, cream paper. Outline ink in warm charcoal or deep indigo, never pure black. Saturation kept under 60%.

**Typography**: Mix of friendly geometric sans and one hand-lettered marker. Reference faces — Brandon Grotesque, GT Walsheim, Recoleta for set type; secondary hand-lettered marker for emphasis words. Wobble in baseline acceptable. Underlines drawn by hand.

**Medium**: Procreate / iPad illustration with textured brush strokes, visible pencil-tooth grain, occasional fill that overshoots its outline by 2–3px, single-weight ink contours. Shadows minimal, sometimes a cross-hatch sketch.

**Composition**: Loose centred subject with hand-drawn arrow and label, headline at the top in marker, supporting doodles (stars, hearts, squiggles, small clouds) scattered casually, slide number circled by hand.
