# Editorial B&W photography

**Vibe**: Long-form magazine reportage — the visual register of a Magnum essay or a New Yorker profile, observed rather than staged, the moment held one beat longer than comfortable.

**Palette**: Black, near-black, three steps of warm grey, paper-white. Single image-level contrast curve pushed toward the deep end — blacks at zero, no clipping in highlights, mid-tones lifted gently. Type ink in true black, never charcoal.

**Typography**: Newsprint serif for headlines, sans for captions and credits. Reference faces — Publico, Lyon Display, Tiempos for serif; Graphik or Söhne for sans. Drop cap optional on the first slide. Caption text in 8–10pt italic.

**Medium**: 35mm photographic with visible silver grain, single-source hard light or even overcast, candid framing, no posed eye-contact. When illustrated, switch to single-weight black ink wash on cream.

**Composition**: Tight crops on faces and hands, wide environmental establishing shots, headline overlaid in a quiet corner with a thin rule above the caption, page-number indicator bottom-right. Asymmetric balance — heavy subject left, type right, or vice versa.

**Style anchor (carousel)**:
> Black-and-white editorial photography in the long-form magazine reportage tradition — Leica-style 35mm with visible silver grain, single-source natural light, mid-tones lifted, deep blacks held at zero with no highlight clipping. Subjects observed candidly, never posed for the camera, framing tight on hands and faces or wide on environment. Composition feels asymmetric — subject anchored on one third, type anchored on the opposite corner with a thin horizontal rule separating headline from caption. Typography reads as a newsprint serif for the headline and a small italic sans for the caption, true-black ink, page-number indicator in the bottom corner. Cultural cue — Magnum photo essays of the 1960s–1980s, broadsheet weekend supplement.

**Best for**: longform journalism, op-eds, profile features, founder essays, anniversary retrospectives.

**Avoid for**: party promos, kids brands, gaming, anything cheerful.
