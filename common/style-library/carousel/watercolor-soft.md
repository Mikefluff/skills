# Soft watercolor

**Vibe**: A botanist's field journal left open on a windowsill — pigment blooms drying on cold-press paper, edges feathered into white, pencil lines just visible beneath the wash.

**Palette**: Dusty rose, sage, ochre, slate blue, terra-cotta wash, soft ivory paper. All colours diluted to roughly 30–50% opacity; pure black never appears, replaced by indigo or deep sepia.

**Typography**: Light contrast serif paired with handwritten script accents. Reference faces — Tiempos Headline, Lyon, GT Sectra for serif; a single fountain-pen italic or handwritten script for two-word accents. Tracking generous, sentence case.

**Medium**: Watercolour wash on visible cold-press paper texture, pigment pooling at edges (the wet-on-wet bloom), pencil under-drawing showing through translucent layers, occasional dry-brush stroke at edges.

**Composition**: Centred or off-centred botanical / object study, headline floating on the cream paper field above or beside the subject, lots of breathing room, a small handwritten annotation near the subject as if labelling a specimen.
