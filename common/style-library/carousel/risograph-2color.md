# Risograph 2-color

**Vibe**: A zine printed in the basement of an art-school co-op — two passes through a Riso machine, the pink plate not quite aligned with the navy plate, paper soft enough to dent under a thumbnail.

**Palette**: Fluorescent pink (F-Pink Riso 22), federal blue (Riso navy), occasional substitution of fluorescent orange or teal. Paper white as third value. Solid blocks of colour with visible grain; pure black does not exist — deepest tone is the overlap of pink and navy producing eggplant.

**Typography**: Art-school confident — geometric grotesque or a quirky display face. Reference faces — GT America, ABC Diatype, Söhne Breit; secondary quirky face like Lapture Display or a hand-set Letraset facsimile. Slight ink misregistration on type intentional.

**Medium**: Two-pass risograph print on uncoated newsprint — visible ink texture with white speckle, half-millimetre plate misregistration between the two colours, occasional roller streaks, paper grain showing through solids.

**Composition**: Bold blocky layout, hero shape filling 60% of the frame, headline overlapping the hero in the opposite ink with misregistered halo, caption stacked at the lower edge, slide number set in a circle that has wandered off-grid by 2mm.
