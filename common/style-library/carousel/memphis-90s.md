# Memphis 90s

**Vibe**: A Trapper Keeper opened on a school desk in 1991 — squiggles, confetti shapes, half-grids and zig-zags shouting at each other across a mint-green field, nothing matching on purpose.

**Palette**: Hot pink, mint green, electric blue, lemon yellow, tangerine, plus pure black for outlines and pure white for negative space. Every shape uses 4–6 of these at once.

**Typography**: Chunky display sans paired with handwritten marker. Reference faces — Tusker Grotesk, Druk Wide, F37 Bolton; secondary handwritten marker for accent words. Letters tilt randomly, drop shadows in a contrasting flat colour offset 4–6px.

**Medium**: Flat vector with intentional scatter — squiggles, grids, dots, half-circles, zig-zags drifting across the field. No gradients, no realism. Occasional terrazzo speckle pattern as background fill.

**Composition**: Wild asymmetry, hero element off-centre, supporting confetti shapes floating around it, headline tilted across the top with a contrasting flat drop shadow, slide number inside a hand-drawn circle.
