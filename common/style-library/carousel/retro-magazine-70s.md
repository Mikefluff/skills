# 70s magazine ad

**Vibe**: The back-cover advert nobody admits they remember — flared collars, ironic deadpan, a paragraph of body copy nobody was supposed to read, the print smell of glossy cigarette-magazine paper.

**Palette**: Burnt orange, harvest gold, avocado green, walnut brown, off-white newsprint, occasional bright cherry red. Slight CMYK misregistration, magenta plate shifted half a millimetre.

**Typography**: Chunky serif display in the headline, condensed serif body. Reference faces — Cooper Black, Windsor, Souvenir, ITC Garamond for headlines; Times or Plantin for body copy. Headline often italic, often huge, often with a small subhead beneath in a contrasting weight.

**Medium**: Kodachrome 64 photographic plate with warm cast, slight halftone visible at scale, occasional die-cut illustration layered on top. Visible paper grain and faint yellowing around edges.

**Composition**: Magazine spread structure — hero image fills the top two thirds, headline drops across the middle, body copy stacked in two columns at the bottom, small product logo in the corner. Heavy text-to-image ratio compared to modern aesthetics.
