# Y2K chrome

**Vibe**: A 2001 motion-graphics intro frozen at the perfect frame — liquid chrome blob spinning over a frosted-pink gradient, the entire screen confident that the future starts on Tuesday.

**Palette**: Polished chrome (cool silver with blue undertones), candy pink, baby blue, lilac, mint, with one acid yellow accent. Backgrounds either gradient pink-blue-purple or frosted translucent white.

**Typography**: Pillowy display sans and squished techno faces. Reference faces — Microgramma, Eurostile, ITC Avant Garde Gothic; secondary chrome-effect display for hero word. Tracking very tight, letters often given a chrome bevel and a small lens-flare specular highlight.

**Medium**: 3D render with hyper-glossy chrome material, reflective specular highlights, soft frosted-glass overlays, anamorphic horizontal lens flare. Backgrounds blurred radial gradients. Occasional pixel-grid pattern background like an early DVD menu.

**Composition**: Hero object centred or floating off-centre, secondary smaller chrome shapes orbiting, headline curved or arched, lens flare across one corner, small star-sparkle ornaments scattered.
