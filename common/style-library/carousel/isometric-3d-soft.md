# Soft isometric 3D

**Vibe**: A fintech app's onboarding tour rendered as a tiny diorama — clay-modelled tiles floating against a pastel sky, every corner rounded enough to be touchable.

**Palette**: Pastel lavender background, blush pink, mint, soft butter yellow, sky blue, one deeper aubergine or indigo for grounding. Highlights stay above 60% lightness; no pure black.

**Typography**: Geometric rounded sans paired with a confident weight contrast. Reference faces — Söhne, Inter, Circular, Manrope, GT Walsheim. Headline at medium or semibold, numerals tabular, caption regular. Often paired with one mono accent for code-ish labels.

**Medium**: 3D render in 30-degree axonometric projection, claymation surface treatment, soft ambient occlusion, subtle subsurface scattering on lighter shapes. No hard reflections; matte finish dominates.

**Composition**: Single hero structure floating mid-frame at a 30-30-30 isometric angle, ground shadow softened to a pastel oval, small satellite objects orbiting at varying altitudes, headline anchored top-left or bottom-right with a horizontal rule.
