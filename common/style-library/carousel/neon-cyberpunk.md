# Neon cyberpunk

**Vibe**: A wet Tokyo alleyway at 2am, signage in three languages competing for attention, the sound of an air conditioner dripping onto a vending machine — high-contrast night, every light source bleeding through the rain.

**Palette**: Magenta-pink (#FF2EA6 register), electric cyan (#00E5FF), deep midnight navy, neon green secondary, black background, occasional acid yellow. Saturation pushed to the wall; mid-tones crushed.

**Typography**: Display tech-sans paired with monospace caption. Reference faces — Druk Wide, NB Architekt, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono. Headline often in all-caps with a slight glitch or chromatic-aberration offset. Numbers in monospace, often with a leading zero.

**Medium**: Photographic with heavy neon practical lighting, anamorphic horizontal lens flares, rain streaks on glass, volumetric haze. When illustrated, switch to flat vector with hard cyan-magenta colour separation.

**Composition**: Low-angle or eye-level urban frames, signage acting as primary light source, wet asphalt reflecting colour, headline placed in negative space between light sources, optional scanline overlay or thin horizontal interlace.
