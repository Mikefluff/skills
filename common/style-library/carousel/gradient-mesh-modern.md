# Modern gradient mesh

**Vibe**: A late-night devtool launch page — auroral gradient blooming behind a glass card, the kind of hero image that says we are serious about infrastructure and we also know what looks good.

**Palette**: Indigo to violet to magenta mesh, with a deeper navy or near-black base. Secondary accents in cyan, lime, or coral. Background generally dark (12–18% lightness); type stays at high-contrast white or pale lavender.

**Typography**: Modern grotesque sans with one subtle mono pair. Reference faces — Inter, Söhne, GT Walsheim, Aeonik for primary; JetBrains Mono or Berkeley Mono for caption. Headline at medium or semibold, tight tracking, large display sizes. Numerals tabular.

**Medium**: Generative gradient mesh — multiple soft colour blobs blended across the canvas, no harsh edges, slight grain overlay to prevent banding. Glass-card UI elements with low-opacity fills and 1px stroke borders. No literal photography.

**Composition**: Hero glass card centred or off-centre, gradient mesh bleeding to all edges, faint grid lines in the background suggesting an underlying CAD plane, headline placed on or just above the glass card with mono caption beneath.
