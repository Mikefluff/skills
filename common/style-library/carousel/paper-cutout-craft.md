# Paper cutout craft

**Vibe**: A picture book opened on a sunlit kitchen table — torn coloured paper layered into shapes, scissor marks visible, glue ridges catching the light.

**Palette**: Mustard, terracotta, ocean blue, leaf green, soft cream background, deep navy or burgundy for outline ink. All papers slightly desaturated, like they have been in a drawer for ten years.

**Typography**: Friendly humanist sans for headlines, hand-lettered or chunky slab for accents. Reference faces — Brandon Grotesque, Avenir Next, Recoleta for warm contrast; sketchy hand-letter for one or two words. Slight rotation on key words, never grid-perfect.

**Medium**: Layered torn-and-cut paper photographed from directly above with soft side-lighting, slight shadow under each paper layer (1–2mm offset), visible paper fibre and grain, occasional pencil mark left in place.

**Composition**: Centered hero subject built from 3–7 paper layers, headline curved or stacked along a paper strip, supporting elements (paper stars, paper leaves, paper dots) scattered with intentional imbalance, cream background filling the rest.
