# B&W line sketch

**Vibe**: A New Yorker spot illustration that arrived twenty minutes before deadline — single-weight pen line on cream paper, no fill, the joke or the idea carried entirely by contour.

**Palette**: Cream paper white (slight warm cast), pure black pen ink. One restrained accent — a single muted red or burnt sienna — used at most twice per carousel. Otherwise monochrome.

**Typography**: Old-style serif with editorial restraint paired with one quiet sans for caption. Reference faces — Caslon, Sabon, Lyon Text for set type; Söhne or Inter Light for caption. Drop cap on slide one. Italic for accents.

**Medium**: Single-weight 0.5mm-equivalent pen line on slight cream paper grain, no shading except occasional fine cross-hatch in deep shadow, no fills, no colour blocks. Lines confident, sometimes overshooting at the join.

**Composition**: Generous cream field (70% empty), small line illustration anchored to one third, headline as serif display set in the remaining negative space, thin horizontal rule separating caption from headline, slide number in lowercase roman numerals in the lower outer corner.
