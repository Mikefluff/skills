# Faded Polaroid

**Vibe**: A shoebox of unsorted photographs from a summer in 1981 — corner curling, magenta light leak across one edge, the people in the picture slightly out of focus and already lost.

**Palette**: Faded ivory paper border, warm pastel skin tones, milky blue sky, muted ochre and dusty rose, occasional magenta or amber light leak. Contrast lifted in shadows, highlights softened.

**Typography**: Handwritten Sharpie or felt-tip on the white Polaroid border, paired with a modest serif for set type. Reference faces — Times Italic, Lyon Text, Tiempos for the rare set type; primary text is hand-written. Date stamp in cheap 7-segment LCD style optional.
