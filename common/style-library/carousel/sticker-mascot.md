# Sticker mascot

**Vibe**: A pet app's onboarding screen blown up to billboard size — a single chunky-outlined character doing one expressive thing per slide, instantly identifiable, designed to live equally well on a phone screen and a tote bag.

**Palette**: One bold brand primary (kelly green, tangerine, cobalt, lipstick red), supporting cream or soft butter background, deep navy or near-black for outline ink, one cheek-blush accent (warm pink) on the character.

**Typography**: Rounded geometric sans paired with one playful display. Reference faces — Circular, Manrope, GT Walsheim, Recoleta for set type; one rounded display like Tusker Rounded or a custom-feel marker for emphasis. Bouncy baseline acceptable on one accent word.

**Medium**: Vector illustration with a thick 3–5px outer outline around every shape (creating the sticker-die-cut effect), flat fills, optional 1–2px inner highlight for dimension. The whole composition reads as if it could be peeled off the slide.

**Composition**: Single hero character centred or off-centred, performing one clear action per slide (waving, pointing, holding a sign), headline on a hand-drawn banner or speech bubble, slide number inside a hand-drawn star or circle.
