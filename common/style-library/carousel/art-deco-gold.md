# Art Deco gold

**Vibe**: A 1925 cocktail menu at a hotel that no longer exists — gold-leaf sunbursts radiating from a central monogram, lacquered black behind it, the velvet of the booth catching ceiling light.

**Palette**: Deep lacquer black, midnight navy, oxblood, ivory, and gold foil (warm yellow gold with brass undertones, not rose gold). Occasional jade green or peacock blue accent.

**Typography**: Geometric display serifs and engraved capitals. Reference faces — Broadway, ITC Anna, Bodoni Poster, Engravers MT for headlines; Trajan or wide-set Times for caption. Letterforms tall, narrow, all-caps for hero, tracked very wide. Drop cap with a foil flourish.

**Medium**: Vector with gold-foil treatment — a metallic gradient with hard specular highlights, not a flat yellow. Subjects rendered as stylised geometric icons (sunburst, fan, ziggurat, pheasant) inside ornate symmetric borders. Subtle linen or velvet texture underlay.

**Composition**: Strict vertical symmetry, central medallion or sunburst as hero, headline arched or stacked beneath, decorative rule frame around the slide edge, small numeric indicator inside a foil cartouche bottom-centre.

**Style anchor (carousel)**:
> Art Deco poster aesthetic of the 1920s — lacquer black field with gold-foil ornament, the gold rendered as a true metallic gradient with hard specular highlights, not a flat yellow. Palette restricted to lacquer black, midnight navy, oxblood, ivory, with one jade or peacock-blue accent. Composition built on strict vertical symmetry — a central sunburst or stepped-ziggurat medallion as the hero, decorative geometric border framing the entire slide, small foil cartouche bottom-centre holding the slide number. Typography reads as tall narrow geometric display caps (Broadway, ITC Anna, Bodoni Poster), tracked wide, with a foil drop cap and a fine engraved-style caption beneath. Cultural cue — Parisian and New York advertising posters between 1920 and 1935.
