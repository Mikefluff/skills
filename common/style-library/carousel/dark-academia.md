# Dark academia

**Vibe**: A reading room in a Bodleian-adjacent library at dusk — leather spines reflecting candlelight, dust motes hanging in a single shaft of late-October sun, an annotated manuscript open on a green leather desk.

**Palette**: Oxblood leather, forest green, oxidised brass, parchment cream, deep sepia, smoke grey. Highlights tinted warm amber from imagined candlelight. Pure black reserved for ink only.

**Typography**: Old-style serif with classical proportions, paired with italic for ornament. Reference faces — Garamond, Caslon, Tiempos, Lyon Text for body; Garamond Premier Italic for accents. Drop caps in the first slide. Roman numerals for slide indicators.

**Medium**: Photographic with deep chiaroscuro lighting, slight film grain, golden-hour warmth, occasional inset of handwritten manuscript or ink-on-parchment detail. When illustrated, switch to single-tone sepia etching.

**Composition**: Single light source from frame-left or upper-left, subject emerging from deep shadow, headline placed in the lit zone with parchment-style underlay, page indicator as roman numeral in lower-outer corner.
