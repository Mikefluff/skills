# Kinfolk minimal

**Vibe**: Slow-living editorial — the visual equivalent of an unhurried Sunday in a Copenhagen apartment, soft daylight and an unmade linen bed.

**Palette**: Oat, bone, warm taupe, faded sage, single charcoal accent. No saturated color above 20% saturation.

**Typography**: High-contrast serif lockup paired with sparse sans caption. Reference faces — Tiempos Headline, Canela Deck, GT Sectra for display; Söhne or Inter Light for caption work. Generous tracking on small caps, sentence case on body, never bold.

**Medium**: 35mm photographic plates with shallow depth of field, natural window light from one direction, micro-grain. When illustrated, switch to single-weight pencil sketch on cream paper.

**Composition**: Aggressive negative space (60% of the canvas empty), single hero object placed off-center on a thirds line, gutter at the top reserved for the headline, caption tucked into the lower-left corner.

**Best for**: thought-leadership posts, founder narratives, brand storytelling, premium lifestyle SKUs.

**Avoid for**: loud sales pitches, fast promo, sports brands, gaming, anything with urgency.
