# Low-poly 3D

**Vibe**: An indie game pitch deck from 2013 — flat-shaded polygons catching directional light, every surface a triangle, the whole world built from clearly visible facets.

**Palette**: Saturated but flat — teal, coral, mustard, plum, deep slate background. Each polygon face gets one solid colour with a single brightness step for the light side. No textures, no gradients within faces.

**Typography**: Geometric sans paired with monospace numerals. Reference faces — Inter, Söhne, Aktiv Grotesk for primary; IBM Plex Mono or Space Mono for caption. Headline at medium, sentence case, slight tracking-out. Slide number always in mono.

**Medium**: Flat-shaded 3D render — no smoothing groups, every triangle face visible, single directional light from upper-left, faceted shadow on the opposite side, no reflections, no ambient occlusion beyond a simple contact shadow.

**Composition**: Single hero polygonal subject floating on a flat coloured field, headline anchored to one corner, faceted ground plane optional, scattered low-poly satellites (crystals, mountains, trees) reinforcing the geometric language.
