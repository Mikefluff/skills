# Brutalist poster

**Vibe**: A photocopied zine taped to a gallery wall in Berlin — typography stacked at wrong angles on purpose, edges chewed, the design refusing to flatter the viewer.

**Palette**: Off-white photocopy paper, true black, one shock colour per piece (acid yellow, hot pink, blood red, or radioactive green). Toner gradients visible. No more than three values per slide.

**Typography**: Hard contrast between a system mono and a deformed display face. Reference faces — IBM Plex Mono, Space Mono, ABC Diatype for body; Druk Cond, Compacta, or any heavy distorted display for headlines. Letters often stretched horizontally, overlapping, baseline-shifted into chaos.

**Medium**: Xerox-of-a-xerox texture, visible toner dust, occasional paper jam streaks, hand-cut tape and staple marks. Photographic elements treated as harsh 1-bit dither.

**Composition**: Intentional grid violation — headline stacked at 7-degree tilt, body text crashing into the edge, overlapping layers of type and image, scribble corrections in red marker. Page numbers slammed into a corner without padding.
