# APPLAUSE LEDGER (EVIDENCE GAP)

## Reference orientation

The slide IS the contradiction: hot stage spotlight + smoke + ecstatic-crowd atmosphere on the top layer (the testimonial wall, the marathon-fan ecstasy, the 5-star burst), and cold accountant ledger + blank data cells + sample-size warnings on the bottom layer (the missing evidence). The aesthetic borrows the visual posture of marketing testimonial-walls used as fake case studies, then exposes them with the cold visual register of peer-reviewed research where the primary data is missing.

## When NOT to use

- Sincere community celebration / actual review collation for genuine products — would read as cynical of all positive feedback. Use `glamour` or `streetwear` instead.
- Concert / event posters for actual concerts — wrong register, the style mocks the form.
- Anything where the data IS available — the style assumes the missing-data is the punchline.

## Variation hints

- **Top/bottom layer discipline**: hot-spotlight atmosphere ALWAYS on top (the lie). Cold-ledger always on bottom (the unmasking). Never invert; that breaks the conceptual premise.
- **5-star bursts work better than literal stars**: render 5-star ratings as decorative starburst graphics around speech bubbles, not as raw "★★★★★" character strings. The visual burst IS the proof-language being mocked.
- **Sample-size "n = ?" / "n = 0" do the rhetorical work**: every testimonial cluster gets a cold sample-size annotation. The contrast between "1500 восторженных отзывов" big-numeral on top and "n = 0 проверяемых кейсов" small-mono on bottom is the heart of the style.
- **Character role**: character is the COLD STATISTICIAN — holding a clipboard, a ledger book, a calculator, or a peer-review form. NEVER one of the ecstatic fans being mocked. The character looks at the testimonial-wall with a "the noise is loud but the numbers are zero" expression.
- **Use stamps sparingly**: "ATMOSPHERE-PROOF", "FEEL ≠ RESULT", "PROOF: PENDING", "n = 0" — one or two per slide, not on every element. The cold-empty look does most of the work; stamps are emphasis.

## Real-world references for orientation only

Think the aesthetic of music-festival testimonial wall colliding with a clinical-trial research notebook — the carnival vs the laboratory, on the same canvas. The style does NOT mimic any specific publication or brand; it borrows the *posture* of exposing emotional reviews marketed as evidence.

Do NOT inject named coaches, named marathon programs, or named brands into image prompts. Keep references in this file only.
