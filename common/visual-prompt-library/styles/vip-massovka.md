# VIP / МАССОВКА (EXCLUSIVITY DEFACED)

## Reference orientation

Looks like exclusivity marketing — gold-foil VIP membership cards, red velvet ropes, "MEMBERS ONLY" embossed emblems, exclusive-club spotlight aesthetic — but every "exclusive" prop is **exposed as mass-produced**. The cardholder is one of 12,847 batched members. The velvet rope opens to an identical headshot grid. The "elite community" Telegram chat is full of identical "привет, я на пути к миллиону" platitudes from generic avatars. The aesthetic IS the unmasking: gold-foil lies on the bottom, red-velvet receipt-mono truths on top.

## When NOT to use

- Genuine luxury / actual exclusive brand work — would read as parody and damage the brand. Use `art-deco` or `glamour` instead.
- Sincere community-building content — too cynical. Use `nature` or `nordic-minimal`.
- Wellness / spiritual brands — wrong register entirely.

## Variation hints

- **Gold-foil ALWAYS bottom layer**: the gold / champagne / VIP-card / velvet-rope elements are the marketing lie — always the ground. Red velvet / batch stamps / queue numbers / receipt mono are the unmasking — always on top.
- **Queue numbers do the rhetorical work**: "ВЫ #4731 / ИЗ 12,847" big-numeral composition is the heart of the style. Use queue numbers / batch tags aggressively — they expose the mass-production hiding behind the exclusivity language.
- **Identical-headshot grid**: when showing the "elite community", render it as a grid of 16–24 nearly-identical headshots (same hairstyle, same coworking-selfie energy, slight variations only). The visual collision IS the joke.
- **Telegram chat mockup discipline**: include 5–8 identical/near-identical messages from different avatars — "привет, я на пути к миллиону", "коллеги, нетворкаемся", "доброе утро, сообщество", "идём к миллиарду", "благодарю за разрешение", etc. The chat IS the evidence.
- **Character role**: character is the SKEPTICAL CARDHOLDER (holding the VIP card with raised eyebrow), or the BOUNCER WHO LET IN EVERYONE (knowing it's not exclusive), or the AUDITOR pointing at the checklist. NEVER one of the duped batched members.

## Real-world references for orientation only

Think the aesthetic of MLM / "elite mastermind" pitch decks — gold-foil VIP cards, premium spotlights, members-only club marketing — colliding with reality (mass-membership chats, identical-content posts, infinite "я на пути к миллиону" messages). The style does NOT mimic any specific publication's house style; it borrows the *posture* of exposing batch-membership marketed as selective access.

Do NOT inject named exclusive clubs, named MLM brands, or named coaches into image prompts. Keep references in this file only.
