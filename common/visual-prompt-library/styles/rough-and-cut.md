# ROUGH AND CUT (DIAMOND DOCTRINE)

## Reference orientation

The aesthetic IS the doctrine — a gemmologist's identification bench in low ambient light with a focused brass task-lamp. The rhetorical wedge is the SORTING ACT: a dusty pile of rough stones, each tied with a small handwritten paper-tag of grievance ("мне должны были помочь" / "обещали поддержать") vs a single cut and polished diamond on a black velvet bed labeled with the three sentences of a mature adult ("это моя ответственность / это моя ошибка / это мой результат"). The style does not insult the rough stones — it merely identifies them, stamps the certificate, and moves on. Cold gemmologist's prudence, not contempt. Think Antwerp diamond-trader's workshop crossed with a Tarkovsky-still patience.

## When NOT to use

- Hype / urgency / startup content — too slow and contemplative.
- Aggressive critique / debunk — use `grunge` or `brutalist` instead; this style judges quietly, not theatrically.
- Wellness / self-help affirmations — the style is gemmological, not therapeutic.
- The actor's-doctrine / corporate-accountability angle — use `ownership-ledger` instead; that's contract-paper, this is gemmology.

## Variation hints

- **The loupe is the rhetorical heart**: every assertion should anchor visually either UNDER the loupe (revealing inclusions, magnifying grievance-tags) or ON the velvet bed (the cut diamond, sealed and clean). Without the loupe vs the velvet, the style collapses.
- **Paper-tag grievances do most of the language work**: each rough stone gets a small handwritten paper-tag with a real-language grievance ("мне должны были помочь" not "GRUDGE LABEL"). The specificity is the rhetoric.
- **Diamond on velvet ALWAYS clean**: the cut diamond gets ZERO labels except its three-sentence verdict tag and a brass certification plaque. No stamps, no inclusions visible. The contrast is the doctrine.
- **Scale-balance for the asymmetry**: when comparing weight, the rough pile on one pan vs a single cut diamond on the other — and the diamond outweighs. The math of the metaphor.
- **Character role**: character is the SENIOR GEMMOLOGIST / DIAMOND APPRAISER / IDENTIFICATION EXPERT seated at the bench under the task-light, loupe in hand, brass tweezers laid across the velvet. Quiet weight of someone who has sorted decades of stones. NEVER smiling — only the patient gravity of identification work. Cold-eyed, task-lamp-lit, three-quarter pose.

## Real-world references for orientation only

Think the aesthetic of an Antwerp diamond-trader's workshop bench circa 1962 — black velvet, brass loupe, focused task-light, certificate paper with embossed seals. Or a Tarkovsky still-life of a jeweler's bench. The style does NOT mimic any specific trader, brand, or certificate house — it borrows the *posture* of patient identification work under a focused lamp.

Do NOT inject named diamond houses, named graders (GIA / HRD / etc.), named jewelers, or named luxury brands into image prompts. Keep references in this file only.
