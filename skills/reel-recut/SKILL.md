<objective>
Finish footage that already exists. Input: a folder of clips in which a person talks to camera (shot, or generated by an avatar model) and that look unfinished: one static framing, no captions, no graphics, no cutaways, sometimes two takes of the same text. Output: one reel per topic at 1080x1920, ready to post, plus a cover for each.

The edit is described in one `plan.json`. Claude writes it from the transcripts; `common.runners.cli.reel_recut` renders it with ffmpeg and headless Chrome. Editing itself calls no paid API. The transcripts, cutaways and music bed come from the generation skills and are the only cost.

Distinct from:

- `reel-builder`: generates the video from a topic. This skill never generates the speaker; the footage is the user's.
- `subtitle-burner`: burns one caption track from an SRT. This skill makes the whole edit, and its captions are timed per word.

This skill does NOT:

- Change what the speaker says, re-voice or lip-sync. A wrong line is cut or the clip is re-shot.
- Remove a watermark baked into the source.
- Edit horizontal or square video. The layout is built for 9:16.
- Post anywhere. That is `post-publisher`.
</objective>

## ROLE

Transcribe every clip with word timings → find duplicate takes and pick one → write `plan.json` (cuts, graphics, cutaways, covers) → generate the cutaways and the music bed → `check` → `build --preview` → look at the contact sheets → fix the plan → final `build`.

## PIPELINE

1. **Ask before building**, in one round: what the cutaways may be (generated stills, generated clips, graphics only), what goes on the end card (site, channel), whether there is music, whether the covers carry an episode number. Take the brand (fonts, colours, wordmark) from the user's site if they name one.

2. **Transcribe** each clip with word timings into `words/<clip>.json`:

   ```bash
   python3 -m common.runners.cli.transcribe --input src/v01.mp4 --format verbose_json --words --lang ru --output words/v01.json --yes
   ```

3. **Pick the takes**:

   ```bash
   python3 -m common.runners.cli.reel_recut takes --words words/
   ```

   It prints the clips that say the same thing, fullest first, and any line said twice in a row inside one clip. Keep the fullest take at the highest resolution; a clip that ends mid-thought is closed with a `tail` (see the plan format). Tell the user which takes were dropped and why.

4. **Write `plan.json`**: one entry per reel. Format and every item kind are in [references/plan-format.md](references/plan-format.md); the rules that decide what goes where are in [references/editing-rules.md](references/editing-rules.md). All times are seconds on the ORIGINAL clip, read off the transcript.

5. **Make the assets** the plan names, into `assets/`: clips through `video-prompt --execute`, stills through `image-prompt --execute`, one music bed through `music-prompt --execute` (60 s, instrumental). Confirm the total cost once. When a video provider is out of balance, a still is a valid cutaway: the builder gives it a slow push.

6. **Check, free**:

   ```bash
   python3 -m common.runners.cli.reel_recut check --plan-file plan.json
   ```

   Missing files and bad items are listed by episode. For each reel it prints what was removed, the framing changes and every caption. Read the captions here: a misheard word is fixed with `word_fix` before anything is rendered.

7. **Preview and look**:

   ```bash
   python3 -m common.runners.cli.reel_recut build --plan-file plan.json --preview
   python3 -m common.runners.cli.reel_recut sheet --plan-file plan.json
   ```

   Open every contact sheet and every cover. The checklist is in [references/editing-rules.md](references/editing-rules.md#review). Fix the plan and rebuild only the episodes that changed with `--only 4 7`.

8. **Final build**: the same `build` without `--preview`.

9. **Report**: where the reels and covers are, which takes were chosen, what was cut, what could not be done (a provider that failed, a watermark left in), and what the user should check by ear, since the mix and the caption wording were verified by eye only.

## CONSTRAINTS

- **Times are on the original clip.** The builder moves them after trimming. Do not recompute a time because a cut was added before it.
- **The speaker's words are not rewritten.** A graphic repeats or sharpens what is said in that second. It never adds a claim the speaker did not make. The one exception is a `tail` on a clip that ends mid-thought, and the user is told to check its wording.
- **One brand for the series.** Fonts, colours and wordmark are set once in the plan, never per episode.
- **Every cutaway has a reason in the line it covers.** Two or three per reel; none over the hook's first second.
- **Check, then preview, then final.** A final encode of an unchecked plan is the slowest way to find a typo.
- **Look before reporting done.** A reel is finished when its contact sheet was opened, not when ffmpeg returned 0.
- **Generated cutaways carry no readable text and no flags unless asked for.** Image models invent both, and an invented flag of the wrong country is the mistake a viewer notices first.
- **Never print API keys.**

## INVOCATION HINTS

When the user says any of:

- "edit these raw clips into reels", "finish my talking-head videos"
- "add captions, b-roll and graphics to these videos"
- "there are duplicate takes, pick the best one"
- "make them ready for Instagram, with covers"

RU triggers:

- «перемонтируй ролики», «смонтируй говорящую голову»
- «добавь бироллы, графику и субтитры к этим видео»
- «там повторы, выбери лучший дубль»
- «сделай, чтобы можно было выложить в инстаграм, с обложками»

If the user has no footage yet and wants a reel about a topic, that is `reel-builder`. If they have a finished video and an SRT, that is `subtitle-burner`.
