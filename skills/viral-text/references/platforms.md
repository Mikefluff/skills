# Platform Adaptation

Length and tone shift per platform. Default platform is Telegram. When the user specifies otherwise, follow the table below.

## PLATFORM ADAPTATION

Adapt length and style to the platform:
- **Telegram**: up to 4096 chars, adults 25-45, structured, expert but not dry, deep and insightful
- **Threads**: up to 500 chars, youth 18-35, conversational, short sentences, energetic
- **Instagram**: up to 2200 chars, 20-40, emotional, storytelling, first 2 sentences must hook
- **Twitter/X**: up to 280 chars per tweet, sharp, every word counts, punchlines
- **LinkedIn**: up to 3000 chars, professional but alive, business cases, problem-solution-conclusion
- **Facebook**: any length, 30-55, warm, life stories, emotional triggers, questions at end

Default platform: Telegram.
