## Patronymics

Russian middle names (отчества) are **not transliterated mechanically** in EN/PT-BR.

- **Formal context (rare):** `Артём Сергеевич Вал` → `Artyom Sergeyevich Val` (full form), to preserve the documentary tone.
- **Everyday context:** the patronymic is dropped.
- **When a character addresses Artyom as "Артём Сергеевич":** the EN translation chooses `Mr Val` or `Artyom` depending on the tone of the address. Never literal `"Artyom Sergeyevich"` unless the moment is explicitly formal/bureaucratic.

The auditor flags any EN/PT-BR file that contains a transliterated patronymic in non-formal context as WARNING — likely mechanical transliteration.

## Diminutives — forms of address

Childhood and intimate forms preserve their register.

| Russian form | EN | PT-BR |
|-------------|-----|-------|
| папа | dad | papai |
| папочка | daddy | paizinho |
| пап | pa | pai |
| мама | mum _(BrE — house choice, set by AB)_ | mãe |
| мам | mum _(same, register from context)_ | mãe |

**Rule of use:** do NOT smooth `папа` / `папочка` / `пап` into a single `father` in EN. The three forms carry register information; collapsing them is a loss of voice.

The house choice for `мама` is `mum` (BrE), not `mom` (AmE). This was set by AB and applies across the series — flagged as warning if `mom` appears.

## Stylistic register markers

Russian voice markers that get translated, not dropped:

| RU | EN | PT-BR |
|----|----|-------|
| Ну… | Well… | Bem… |
| Знаете, что… | You know what… | Sabe… |
| Светает (impersonal) | It's getting light (with explicit subject) | está clareando |
| Холодает (impersonal) | It's getting cold | está esfriando |

EN and PT-BR require a subject; Russian impersonal constructions get an "it" / "está" wrapper. Not optional.

## Cultural realia — footnote pattern

Russian-specific places, institutions, products: explanatory footnote on **first mention** in the chapter, none after. The footnote is short, factual, and does not interpret.

| Realia | First-mention footnote (EN) | After |
|---------|------------------------------|--------|
| Лубянка | "Lubyanka, FSB headquarters in central Moscow" | "Lubyanka" |
| Шуховская башня | "Shukhov Tower, hyperboloid radio tower in Moscow" | "Shukhov Tower" |
| Госуслуги (EA) | "Gosuslugi, Russia's e-government portal" | "Gosuslugi" |
| Тинькофф | _(no footnote — brand is recognizable)_ | "Tinkoff Bank" |
| Троллейбус №15 | _(no footnote — context is clear)_ | "Trolleybus 15" |
| Хамовники / Шаболовка / Баррикадная | transliteration + footnote on first mention of district | transliteration only |
| Феназепам | first-mention pharmacological footnote | "Phenazepam" |
| Гастрощит | first-mention pharmacological footnote | "Gastroshield" |

**Failure modes the auditor flags:**

- First mention without footnote → BLOCKING.
- Footnote on every mention (not just first) → WARNING.
- Realia translated descriptively without the proper noun (`"a Moscow radio tower"` instead of `"Shukhov Tower"`) → BLOCKING.
- Brand transcribed wrong (`"Sberbank"` for `Тинькофф`) → BLOCKING.
