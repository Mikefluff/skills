# BANNED CONSTRUCTIONS (поверх writer)

Дополнительно к writer hard bans:

- **Академический пафос**: «как было показано выше», «настоящая работа призвана», «рассмотрим следующий аспект» — выбрасывать
- **Лекторский тон**: «обратите внимание», «отметим, что», «как мы уже сказали» — переписывать через действие или вообще снимать
- **Псевдоучёная вода**: «комплексный подход», «системный взгляд», «целостное понимание» — заменять конкретикой того, что имеется в виду
- **Корпоративная футурология**: «эра ИИ», «цифровая трансформация», «новая нормальность» — снимать как штампы
- **Виральные приёмы в нон-фикшн**: numbered points «1./2./3.», hook-формулы, NLP-вопросы в конце, ==keyword== — НЕ применять
- **Списки flags**: «5 признаков того, что…», «10 правил…» — это форма для соцсетей, не для книги

## EN banned constructions for non-fiction

Parallel hard-bans for English-language essay / book / long-form non-fiction prose. These apply on top of the writer EN AI-style signatures (neuroslop-categories.md, sections EN-1..EN-18). Specifically essay-only — the constructions below are not always wrong in fiction or in viral, but in essay non-fiction they are slop.

- **Balance-both-sides hedges (unjustified)**: "while there are valid points on both sides...", "it's worth considering both perspectives", "the truth lies somewhere in between". If the evidence actually warrants a balanced read, write it concretely — name which point survives, which falls. Banned only when the balance is a non-commitment dodge.

- **Vague-landscape openers**: "In today's complex landscape...", "In an era of unprecedented change...", "As we navigate an increasingly...". Cut entirely; open with the actual claim.

- **Pseudo-academic filler**: "It can be argued that...", "Some would say that...", "One might suggest...". If you can argue it, argue it directly. If you can't, drop the claim.

- **Filler transitions**: "It is interesting to note that...", "It is worth pointing out that...", "It bears mentioning that...". Cut entirely.

- **Author-distancing**: "the present author would suggest...", "one is inclined to think...", "this writer believes...". Use I or we directly. The third-person dodge reads as defensive academic prose.

- **Unjustified triplets**: "X, Y, and Z" where Z is just a synonym of X ("smart, sharp, and intelligent"). Keep triplets only when the three items carry distinct information. (Cross-link: neuroslop-categories.md EN-13 AI_TRIPLETS.)

- **Filler intensifiers**: "ultimately", "fundamentally", "essentially", "at its core", "at the end of the day". Cut on first pass; restore only if the word carries an actual semantic load (e.g. "ultimately" meaning "as the final step in time", not as rhetorical emphasis).

- **Discourse-marker tic at paragraph openers**: "Furthermore,", "Moreover,", "Additionally,", "In addition,", "Of course,", "Indeed,", "Notably," — at most one per essay. Same rule as writer EN-11.

- **"In conclusion" as closer**: ban the literal phrase and its variants ("To summarize", "To conclude", "In summary", "All in all") as the opener of the final paragraph. The reader knows it's the last paragraph; the signpost is amateur. (Cross-link: neuroslop-categories.md EN-12.)

- **AI-tell vocabulary in essay register**: "delve into", "tapestry of", "navigate the complexities of", "embark on a journey", "plays a pivotal role", "cornerstone of", "underscores the importance of", "multifaceted", "intricate interplay". Each is an immediate downgrade to machine-prose status in non-fiction. Cross-link: neuroslop-categories.md EN-2..EN-10.

- **Viral techniques imported into essay**: numbered "1./2./3." lists, hook-formulas, NLP-question close, ==keyword== highlight, bold inside paragraphs — do NOT apply. Same as the RU bullet above; restated here so the EN reviewer doesn't reach for viral patterns by reflex.

- **Listicle titles**: "5 signs that…", "10 rules of…", "The 7 habits of…" — social-media form, not book form. Title the chapter by its claim, not by its count.
